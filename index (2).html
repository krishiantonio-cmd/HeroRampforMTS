The hiring challenge

Technical roles in the Philippines are hard to fill well, and fast

Narrow skill sets

Vendor certifications, L2 and L3 experience, and a second language rarely show up together in one CV.

Readiness is hard to see

A CV says what someone did. It says much less about whether they're ready for this role, this shift and this client.

Time lost to screening

Recruiters spend hours filtering people who were never a fit, while client deadlines keep moving.

What HeroRamp is

An AI-powered hiring and workforce platform that helps employers find, assess, match and hire better-prepared talent.

It connects employers, candidates and talent partners in one system, so the right people reach the right roles with clear reasons behind every match.
